ℹ Introduction

GoShareX ("we", "our", or "us") is a ride-sharing application built exclusively for students of American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the GoShareX mobile application available on Android.

By using GoShareX, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree, please do not use the app.

1 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use GoShareX:

  • Account Information: Full name, Gmail address, student ID, department
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates while the app is active and during rides
  • Ride Data: Ride history, destinations, pickup points, fares, completion status
  • Chat Messages: Messages sent between matched riders (auto-deleted after 24h; anonymous chats after 10 min)
  • Ratings & Reviews: Star ratings and feedback between riders
  • Device Data: Device type, OS version, FCM push notification token
We do NOT collect payment card information, bank details, or financial credentials of any kind.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • Match you with nearby riders going to the same destination
  • Show your real-time location on the live map to matched partners only
  • Send push notifications for ride requests, confirmations, and chat messages
  • Calculate fare splits and display ride savings statistics
  • Maintain your trust score, badge level, and ride history
  • Detect and prevent fraud, harassment, and community guideline violations
  • Enforce account suspensions for repeated cancellations or misconduct

3 Location Data

GoShareX requires access to your device location to provide core functionality:

  • Foreground location: Used while the app is open to show your position and match nearby riders
  • Background location: Used during active rides only, to track ride progress

Your precise location is only shared with your confirmed ride partner — never publicly visible to all users.

You may disable location access in your device settings at any time, but this will prevent the app from functioning properly.

We do not store your historical GPS coordinates permanently. Location data is used in real-time only and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

4 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, realtime, and storage (our backend infrastructure)
  • OpenStreetMap-based services (Photon, GraphHopper): Location search, routing, and directions
  • Other GoShareX users: Only your display name, destination, pickup point, vehicle preference, and rating are visible to nearby riders

Your Gmail address and student ID are never shared with other users.

5 Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • Ride history is kept for 12 months then automatically purged
  • Regular chat messages expire after 24 hours
  • Anonymous chat rooms auto-delete after 10 minutes
  • Real-time location data is not stored permanently
  • Push notification tokens are updated on each login and removed on account deletion
You may delete your account at any time from the Settings screen. This removes your profile, active location, chats, rides, posts, and notification token. We may retain audit logs and other legally required records for compliance and safety.

6 Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS / TLS encryption
  • Row Level Security (RLS) enforces strict access control — users can only access their own data
  • Passwords are securely hashed — we never store or see plaintext passwords
  • Sensitive tokens (auth, push) stored in device secure storage, not plain AsyncStorage
  • Email verification required before account activation
  • Account suspension system for policy violations

7 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information via the app's Edit Profile screen
  • Deletion: Request full deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent: Disable location tracking at any time via device settings
  • Opt out of notifications: Disable push notifications via device settings
  • Data portability: Download a JSON export of your profile, ride history, chats, posts, ratings, and audit logs from the Settings screen

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 7 business days.

Delete Your Account

  • Open the app and go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the request to permanently remove your account and data.

8 Children's Privacy

GoShareX is intended exclusively for university students aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account within 48 hours.

9 Third-Party Services

GoShareX uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, realtime, storage
  • OpenStreetMap / GraphHopper — Location search, routing, directions
